01 — направление

Spatial Depth

Panes of light held apart in the dark

Depth carries the hierarchy so colour does not have to. Every surface sits on a declared z-tier — the hero pane catches the most light and throws the longest shadow, list rows recede, the tab bar floats free of the content behind it. On a flat phone that reads as calm and engineered, and it makes "what is on top of what" legible without drawing a single border.

#0A0E1A
substrate #131A2C
tier 0 #25D366
channel #7CF3AC
positive #F4F7FF
ink
Material
Pane = 6% white fill, 22 px backdrop blur, 1.4 px inset top highlight, 0 20 44 −14 ambient shadow.
Motion
Tiers parallax at 1.0 / 0.72 / 0.45 on scroll. Screens enter by advancing in z, never by sliding in x.
Risk
Dark UI plus live blur is the heaviest option on mid-range Android; needs a pre-baked backdrop.

VerdictThe most premium-feeling of the five, and the most expensive to run.

02 — направление

Liquid Glass

Optics, not opacity — light bending at every edge

What separates this from a frosted white rectangle is the edge: a specular top highlight, a darker inner bottom edge, and a tint that samples whatever sits behind it. Glass belongs to the navigation layer — chips, tab bar, sheets — and never to content. That single rule is what stops the style collapsing into blur soup, and it is the rule most implementations break.

#EAF4FF
ground #6FD4FF
refract A #B8F5C8
refract B #0A5FC8
active #0D1526
ink
Material
Glass = 55% white, 24 px blur, ×1.9 saturation, 1 px #FFF border, inset 1.6 px specular top edge.
Motion
Materials merge and split under gesture. Sheets lift 0.94 → 1.0 over 0.25 s OutBack.
Risk
Text over an unknown backdrop. Requires a mandatory scrim and a Reduce Transparency solid fallback.

VerdictReads current and Apple-adjacent. Legibility discipline is the whole game.

03 — направление

Neumorphism

Controls sculpted out of the surface itself

Raised means available, inset means containing or already pressed — the whole interface becomes one moulded sheet lit from the top-left. Pure neumorphism fails accessibility badly, so what is shown here is the corrected hybrid: soft extrusion for surfaces, but every interactive control keeps a real 1 px border and one saturated accent, so a first-time user can still tell what is tappable.

#E7EBF3
base #FFFFFF
light src #9DA8BE
shadow #1B5CEB
accent #232A3C
ink
Material
Raised = 7 7 15 rgba(157,168,190,.65) plus −7 −7 15 rgba(255,255,255,.98). Inset halves both offsets.
Motion
Press crossfades raised → inset over 0.12 s. That crossfade is the entire personality of the style.
Risk
Base must stay in a narrow light band; it dies on pure white, on black, and on top of photography.

VerdictDistinctive and tactile — but only viable as the corrected hybrid, never pure.

04 — направление

Neo-Skeuomorphism

Materials that admit they are objects

Felt ground, matte-plastic plates, a milled switch, fine grain across the whole screen, one consistent overhead light. The 2010 failure was kitsch — stitched leather on a calendar. The modern version is material honesty: surfaces behave like a substance, but nothing pretends to be an object it is not. Affordance comes free, which matters a great deal for a non-technical owner.

#C9BCA6
felt #FDFAF2
plate #A99C84
edge #1D5C33
action #2E2517
ink
Material
Plate = 3-stop vertical gradient, 1 px #A99C84 edge, inset 1.5 px white top, 0 3 5 cast shadow.
Motion
Weight. A press travels 2 units down and darkens, then releases with a small overshoot. Pair with haptics.
Risk
Highest asset count — grain, bevels and metal need authored sprites, and texture ages fastest.

VerdictThe most memorable and most opinionated. Also the one that ages fastest.

05 — направление

Refined Modern

No material at all — only craft

Nothing here is a gimmick, so everything has to be exact: hairline borders at a true 1 px, neutrals tinted toward the brand blue instead of pure grey, three stacked shadows rather than one, tabular figures wherever numbers align, inner radius derived as outer minus padding. This is the direction that survives Russian string lengths, dark mode, and five years of feature creep.

#FAFBFD
ground #FFFFFF
surface #E7EAF1
hairline #1B5CEB
accent #0D1220
ink
Material
Elevation 2 = 0 1 2 .05 + 0 4 8 −2 .05 + 0 16 28 −12 .09, every layer tinted rgba(16,24,40).
Motion
Restrained: 0.18 s state changes, 0.05 s list stagger, skeleton placeholders instead of spinners.
Risk
Lowest ceiling on distinctiveness — without obsessive detail it lands as default and forgettable.

VerdictThe safe, shippable bar — and the best base to layer one of the other four onto.

Choosing between them

The product truth constrains the aesthetics. A shop owner is handing their real customer conversations to an AI, running on their own phone number. Every direction has to make that feel controlled, not clever. Two of the five carry genuine risk against that goal, and the table says which.
