Refined Modern · восемь палитр · 2026

One material. Eight ways to mean something.

Every screen below is the same Refined Modern build — identical hairlines, identical three-layer elevation, identical radius and type. Only the token file changes. That is the whole promise of the style: a palette is a swap, not a rewrite.

Contrast is computed, not claimed. Each palette lists its measured WCAG ratios; every accent carries a separate verified text shade so it can be a link and a button fill without failing either job. WhatsApp green, Telegram blue and the five outcome-status hues are held constant across all eight — identity and meaning never follow fashion.

Светлые палитры

Five light grounds. The neutral itself is the biggest lever here — cool, achromatic and warm neutrals feel like different products before the accent even lands.

How to pick

Judge the Настройки phone hardest. A dashboard flatters any palette — big numbers, lots of white space. A dense form is where a neutral turns muddy, where a border either reads or disappears, and where an accent has to survive being small.

The neutral matters more than the accent. Cool, achromatic and warm grounds already feel like three different companies before you notice the colour on the button. Pick the ground first, then the accent.

On «Алматы»: if crimson is the brand colour, destructive actions cannot also be red. That palette moves delete to an outlined treatment with a darker maroon, so «Удалить» never wears the same skin as «Сохранить». Any palette whose accent collides with a semantic colour needs this kind of ruling written down.

Once chosen, the palette becomes one token block in Assets/Scripts/Theme/, and every [MenuItem] builder reads it. Swapping later costs one file, not fifty-five.
