Refined Modern · два направления · 7 вариантов

Cool dark and warm paper, past the obvious version.

Both directions you liked, extended — same Refined Modern material, same three screens. The dashboard now shows four statuses instead of three, because both of these accents collide with a colour that already means something, and a mock that hides the collision is useless.

«Графит» has a blue problem: Telegram blue and «В диалоге» are both blue. «Песок» has a green problem, and it is worse: WhatsApp green, the «бот работает» switch and «Заказ собран» are three greens that may never move. Every palette states how it separated the brand from them, and the distance to both channel colours is measured in ΔE.

«Графит» — прохладная тёмная

The cool dark you liked, pushed across its real range. The ground temperature does as much work as the accent: blue-black, slate and violet-black are three different products before the accent lands. Note that a blue brand has to fight Telegram blue and the «В диалоге» status — the non-blue accents sidestep that fight entirely.

How to choose

Judge the Настройки screen and the four status dots together. The dashboard is where a brand that competes with a channel colour or a status gives itself away — if you have to look twice to tell the brand from «Заказ собран», that palette is out no matter how good the form looks.

For «Песок», the real question is whether you liked the sage or the paper. Half the options below keep the green and fight for separation; half drop it entirely and keep the warm ground. Both preserve the calm — only one of them keeps the brand out of WhatsApp's territory.

Whichever wins becomes one token block in Assets/Scripts/Theme/, read by every [MenuItem] builder.
